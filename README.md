# 📊 Employee Performance and Attrition in the IT Sector

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project was developed as part of my **MBA dissertation on Employee Performance and Attrition in the IT Sector**.

The dashboard provides an interactive analysis of employee demographics, job satisfaction, salary satisfaction, managerial feedback, work experience, and their relationship with employee attrition.

The objective of this project is to use **HR data analytics** to identify patterns and factors associated with employee attrition and provide insights that can support better employee retention and workforce management decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze employee attrition patterns across different demographic and professional groups.
* Understand the relationship between **job satisfaction, salary satisfaction, and employee attrition**.
* Examine how **manager feedback** relates to employee attrition.
* Analyze workforce distribution based on **years of experience**.
* Compare attrition patterns across **gender and experience levels**.
* Build an interactive HR dashboard to communicate key findings effectively.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard development
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Measures and analytical calculations
* **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, KPIs, and comparative analysis

## 🔍 Key Areas of Analysis

### 👥 Employee Demographics

The dashboard examines the distribution of respondents by **age group and gender** to understand the composition of the workforce.

### ⭐ Job & Salary Satisfaction

Job satisfaction and salary satisfaction are analyzed against employee attrition to identify potential relationships between employee satisfaction and intention to leave.

### 👨‍💼 Managerial Feedback

Manager feedback ratings are compared with attrition patterns to explore the potential relationship between managerial feedback and employee retention.

### 💼 Work Experience

Employees are segmented according to their years of experience to identify differences in attrition patterns across experience levels.

### 👤 Gender & Attrition

The dashboard compares attrition across gender groups to identify differences in employee retention patterns.

## 💡 Business Value

Employee attrition can be a significant challenge for organizations in the **IT sector**, where skilled employees and technical expertise are important organizational assets.

This project demonstrates how HR data can be transformed into an interactive dashboard that helps stakeholders:

* Identify employee groups with higher attrition levels
* Monitor employee satisfaction
* Understand potential attrition drivers
* Compare workforce segments
* Support employee retention strategies
* Make more informed, data-driven HR decisions
